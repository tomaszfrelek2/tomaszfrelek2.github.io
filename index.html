About Me Hello! I’m a Computer Science grad student at UC San Diego, and I’m all about building smarter autonomous systems. My journey started at The Ohio State University, where I double-majored in Computer Science Engineering and Theoretical Math. That combo gave me a great toolkit for tackling the really tough and interesting problems in AI, computer vision, and robotics.

I love working on the tech that helps autonomous systems see and understand the world! My time as a Radar Perception Engineer at Oculii was a blast—I got to develop transformer models for 3D object detection and work on a autonomous driving stack for radar vision! Before that, I spent a couple of summers at JPMorgan Chase, where I got a deep dive into large-scale software engineering, building high-speed data tools and services that managed petabytes of information for hundreds of developer teams. You can see my full work history on my linkedin (and feel free to add me!)

My research experiences have allowed me to explore some of the most exciting challenges in machine learning. Under the guidance of Dr.JohnPaparrizos, I helped compile a comprehensive library of timeseries classification and forecasting algorithms and implemented novel deep-learning models with PyTorch. In Dr.HarryChao’s computer vision lab, I’ve had the chance to work on two fascinating frontiers: pioneering Imageomics to analyze biological data through machine learning, and tackling the challenge of learning from limited or imperfect data to improve perception systems in autonomous driving. Both experiences have been incredible, allowing me to apply cutting-edge theory to solve tangible, real-world problems.

Recently, I’ve joined Dr.HenrickChristansen’s Cognative Robotics lab to work on the autonomous scooter project. It’s been a lot of fun so far and I’m learning new things every day!

I work on many side projects in my free-time. I enjoy competing in conference competitions, (most recently CVPR 2025), and I’ve also poured a lot of time into collaborative projects like Buckeye AutoDrive (OSU’s self-driving car project), where I worked on the perception team to help our autonomous car see and navigate the world. Most of my projects are available on my github.

When I’m not in front of a computer, I’m probably outside. I like rock-climbing, hiking, playing pickup, walking my dogs (I have 4!). Currently I’m learning how to surf, my balance sucks so let me know if you have any advice!
